### 4. Flood and Glacial-Lake Outburst Scenarios

- The 2024 Thame GLOF originated from a cascade of two small lakes not previously considered dangerous. (empirical)
  > "The GLOF originated from a cascade of two small lakes that had not previously been considered dangerous."
  [abstract-egu25-12752]
- GLOF peak discharge attenuates downstream until it crosses monsoon-flood magnitude. (empirical)
  > "As a GLOF travels downstream, the flood peak attenuates and the peak discharge decreases, whereas the drainage area and background discharge increase"
  [geomorphology]
- Replacing kinematic wave routing with local inertia equation raised peak discharge NSE from 0.48 to 0.71 in Amazon/Ganges tests. (empirical)
  > "replacing the kinematic wave approximation of the hydrologic model with the local inertia equation of CaMa-Flood greatly enhances accuracy of peak discharge simulations as expressed by an increase in the Nash–Sutcliffe efficiency (NSE) from 0.48 to 0.71."
  [nhess-evaluating-the-impact-of-model-complexity-on-flood-wave-propagation-and-in]
- Tsho Rolpa NWS-BREACH peak flows range 8,198 to 26,662 m3/s for 20 and 40 m breaches, then routed in HEC-RAS. (empirical)
  > "This analysis revealed peak flow rates ranging from 8198 to 26 662 m3 s−1 for the respective breaching scenarios."
  [piahs-glacial-lake-outburst-flood-glof-modeling-of-tsho-rolpa-glacial-lake-nepal]
- The 2021 Melamchi flood killed 25 and destroyed MWSP headworks; trigger still debated. (empirical)
  > "killing 25 people, destroying hundreds of houses, and damaging critical infrastructure, including the headworks of the Melamchi Water Supply Project"
  [the-2021-melamchi-flood-a-massive-erosional-cascade-in-the-himalayan-mountains-o]

### 5. Earthquake Scenarios

- Himalayan-region attenuation relations matched Gorkha observed ground motion; other regions' relations did not. (empirical)
  > "The results clearly show that the attenuation relation developed for the Himalayan region should only be used, and other attenuation relations based on other regions fail to provide good estimate of observed ground motion parameters."
  [attenuation-relation-predicted-observed-ground-motion-of-gorkha-nepal-earthquake]
- In the 5 July 2016 Gongbatongshacuo GLOF, the flood front propagated between seismic stations 28-35 km below the Zhangzangbo confluence at 8.7 m/s, with a slower second (coarse-sediment) pulse at 5 m/s. (empirical)
  > "The first is the flood front, which propagates between stations at 8.7 m/s. ... The second pulse is of higher magnitude but travels slower, at 5 m/s."
  [geomorphology]
- The 2020 ICIMOD/UNDP inventory mapped 3,624 glacial lakes in Koshi, Gandaki and Karnali basins, 2,070 in Nepal. (empirical)
  > "study found 3,624 glacial lakes located in the three basins, of which 2,070 lakes are in Nepal"
  [inventory-of-glacial-lakes-and-identification-of-potentially-dangerous-lakes-1]
- 47 lakes are potentially dangerous (21 in Nepal), ranked on lake/dam, glacier and surroundings criteria only; seismic, climate and human triggers excluded. (empirical)
  > "Other factors, such as extreme climatic conditions, seismic activity, and human interference on or around the lake and on the dam, are not considered."
  [inventory-of-glacial-lakes-and-identification-of-potentially-dangerous-lakes-1]
- GEM/BGS publish an open Nepal PGA hazard grid (10% and 2% in 50 yr) at 0.025 deg in CSV. (statistical)
  > "Data Format ASCII CSV (attibutes: lat,long,PGA-0.1,PGA-0.02)"
  [meteor-nepal-seismic-hazard-map-with-mean-peak-ground-acceleration-pga-in-g-for]
- Natural-frequency formats aid probability understanding but lose benefit for low-probability high-consequence events like major earthquakes. (theoretical)
  > "the benefits of using natural frequencies decrease when dealing with low-probability, high-consequence (LPHC) events like major earthquakes, where even significant increases in relative probability remain small in absolute terms."
  [public-understanding-and-scientific-uncertainty-the-communication-of-risk-in-the]
- At Balaju Park (Siddhitol) Gorkha ground motions above ~1 s exceeded Nepal building-code spectra, indicating need for site-specific hazard analysis. (empirical)
  > "The resulting ground motions exceed the design levels from the Nepalese Building Codes, indicating the need for site-specific hazard analysis and for revision of the building code to address the effect of site amplification."
  [site-amplification-in-the-kathmandu-valley-during-the-2015-m76-gorkha-nepal-eart]
- KATNP Gorkha spectra peaked at ~0.4 s and ~5 s, far above code; 475-yr UHS misestimates demand by period. (empirical)
  > "Elastic response spectra of the mainshock near the centre of the Valley (KATNP station operated by USGS) contained two very distinct peaks: a narrower and larger peak at ~0.4 s and a smaller, but very wide, peak at ~5 s."
  [the-2015-mw-78-gorkha-earthquake-in-nepal-and-its-aftershocks-analysis-of-strong]
- Over 70% of flood material passed to Indrawati; lower basin widened 3-5x, aggraded 5-20 m. (empirical)
  > "the lower basin was widened by 3-5-fold and aggraded by ~ 5–20 m"
  [the-2021-melamchi-flood-a-massive-erosional-cascade-in-the-himalayan-mountains-o]

### 7. Printable Reports, Trust and Risk Communication

- Isarithmic (contour-style) maps yield better understanding of hazard-exposure uncertainty than gradational shaded or binned maps. (empirical)
  > "isarithmic maps produce the better understanding of uncertainty about hazard exposure than gradational shaded or binned maps (Thompson et al., 2015 ; Mulder et al., 2017 )."
  [frontiers-improving-hazard-map-comprehension-for-protective-action-decision-maki]
- People's risk judgments follow a proximity heuristic: perceived risk decreases monotonically with distance from the hazard source. (empirical)
  > "people's risk judgments can be characterized by a proximity heuristic (Teigen, 2005 ), which generates a perceived risk gradient that is a monotone decreasing function of distance from a hazard source"
  [frontiers-improving-hazard-map-comprehension-for-protective-action-decision-maki]
- Back-calculated 1941 Palcacocha scenarios give 36-70 min travel to Huaraz and peaks >10,000 m3/s; predictive runs need many back-calculated events and uncertainty handling. (empirical)
  > "Predictive simulations of possible future events have to be based on a larger set of back-calculated GLOF process chains, taking into account the expected parameter uncertainties and appropriate strategies to deal with critical threshold effects."
  [hess-reconstruction-of-the-1941-glof-process-chain-at-lake-palcacocha-cordillera]
- At L'Aquila 2009, official reassurance led many to remain indoors when the M6.3 quake struck. (historical)
  > "These assurances given by officials led many to remain in their homes when the earthquake struck."
  [public-understanding-and-scientific-uncertainty-the-communication-of-risk-in-the]
- Six design strategies for hazard geovisualizations: key data only, audience tailoring/testing, salient cues, emotion, pattern recognition aids, limited distractions. (theoretical)
  > "These strategies include: Displaying only key data, tailoring and testing geovisualizations with the audience, using salient cues, leveraging emotion, aiding pattern recognition, and limiting visual distractions."
  [using-geovisualizations-to-educate-the-public-about-environmental-health]
- Among US residents in FEMA-designated floodplains, 57% perceived lower risk than FEMA estimates and 29% were uncertain. (empirical)
  > "Among residents in FEMA-designated floodplains, 57% perceived a lower risk than FEMA estimates, and 29% expressed uncertainty."
  [uvm-study-reveals-millions-at-risk-college-of]
- Study authors recommend interactive mapping tools and community-based education to bridge expert-public risk perception gaps. (expert-opinion)
  > "Interactive mapping tools and community-based education are recommended to bridge the gap between expert assessments and public perceptions."
  [uvm-study-reveals-millions-at-risk-college-of]
